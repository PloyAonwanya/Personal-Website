About Me

Before taking my first programming class, I appreciated art and design, but I had never seen how those would connect.

Until my Web Programming Technology class, where I built the front end of a restaurant and café recommendation platform for students around the university as part of the class project. Since then, I have focused on front end development.
I later further developed my design skills during a 10-month internship as a Full Stack Developer Intern at Mfec Public Company Limited, which allowed me to learn how to design and build web apps in the real world.

I am now continuing to improve my skills in HTML, CSS, Javascript (and other programming languages) to design web apps and offer users a positive user experience and real value.
